# Theodor Wulff

Doctoral Candidate at the University of Manchester, working on 
chain-of-thought alignment between language and actions in Vision 
Language Action models. I use mechanistic interpretability to 
understand how VLAs ground language in the physical world.

Looking to bring that research foundation into industry as an ML 
Engineer or Applied Scientist.

## Research & Interests
- Vision-Language-Action Models (VLAs): alignment between 
  chain-of-thought reasoning and generated actions
- Language grounding and semantic alignment across vision, 
  language, and embodied action
- Mechanistic interpretability in VLAs and foundation models

## Projects & Publications

- [VLA Understanding](https://github.com/TheodorWu/VLAUnderstanding)  
  Attribution patching to investigate language grounding in VLAs *(ongoing)*
  
- [GPLA](https://theodorwu.github.io/GPLA/)    
  Preference learning framework 
  to align chain-of-thought reasoning with action generation. 
  *Published at CVPR Findings 2026*

- [Action Language Transparency](https://github.com/TheodorWu/ActionLanguageTransparency)  
  Joint generation of action and language tokens to improve 
  interpretability of action generation
  *Published at IJCNN2025*

- [PPO Street Fighter 2](https://github.com/TheodorWu/PPO-SF)     
  PPO-based RL agent trained to play Street Fighter 2 *(older project)*
